The Graduate

Acquaint yourself with Lindsey in preparation for the celebratory tea.

Accomplishments

Accomplishments

With great pride, we present Miss Lindsey, whose accomplishments reflect both diligence and grace. She has distinguished herself in Zion’s Youth and the esteemed ranks of Honor and All State Choir, and has frequently graced her church with musical performances. Having graduated from Nevada State High School, she continues to demonstrate industry through her steady service at the Montessori and her refined skill as a professional nail artist.

Likes

Likes

Miss Lindsey is likewise known for her lively and amiable disposition, finding great delight in song, in which she is most accomplished, and in the cheerful laughter she so readily inspires. She treasures the company of dear friends, enjoys the delicate art of painting nails, and takes pleasure in the consumption of quality and engaging media.

Dislikes

Dislikes

Though Miss Lindsey is not particularly fond of injuries, tiresome homework, unnecessary drama, or the occasional unruly child at her place of work, she meets each with remarkable composure. Indeed, she bears such inconveniences with admirable grace and a cheerful spirit, managing all with a poise and good humor that only further endears her to those around her.

Goals

Goals

Miss Lindsey’s aspirations are no less admirable than her accomplishments, for she seeks to obtain formal distinction as a nail artist, to serve faithfully as a missionary for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and to pursue the noble calling of music therapy. She is, additionally, delightfully resolved to acquire a most proper French accent.
