The Schedule

Order of the Afternoon's Engagements

Arrival and Courteous Greetings

Guests arrive and exchange polite greetings in a most cordial manner.

Tea and Light Refreshments Served

Tea and delicate refreshments are served and enjoyed in pleasant company.

Croquet upon the Lawn

Attendees partake in a spirited yet genteel game of croquet upon the lawn.

Musical Performances and Entertainment

Select guests provide musical performances for the enjoyment of all present.

Conversation and Farewells

The gathering concludes with warm conversation and gracious farewells.
